                   In this Letter, it is demonstrated using two-ﬂuid simulations that low-β magnetic reconnection remains fast,
                    regardless of the presence of fast dispersive waves, which have been previously suggested to play a critical
                       role. To understand these results, a discrete model is constructed that oﬀers scaling relationships for the
                   reconnection rate and dissipation region (DR) thickness in terms of the upstream magnetic ﬁeld and DR
                    length. We verify these scalings numerically, and show how the DR self-adjusts to process magnetic ﬂux at
                   the same rate that it is supplied to a larger region where two-ﬂuid eﬀects become important. The rate is
                    therefore independent of the DR physics, and is in good agreement with kinetic results.
