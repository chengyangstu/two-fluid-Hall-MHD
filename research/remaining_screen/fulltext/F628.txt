         Earth’s magnetotail have been postulated as a possible

              It is well known that during magnetic reconnection a
         quadrupolar out-of-plane or Hall magnetic ﬁeld is formed
        by the ﬁeld aligned currents that ﬂow in the vicinity of
         the magnetic separatrices when ion and electrons decou-
          ple on the length scales less than the ion inertial length
           (di)  [4].  These Hall ﬁelds and their associated mag-
          netic ﬂux are swept from the reconnection layers super-
           Alfv´enically by kinetic Alfv´en waves (KAW) [5, 6]. Ob-
          servations from Cluster spacecraft have conﬁrmed that
         such waves are indeed generated by magnetic reconnec-
          tion and play a vital role in transporting energy [7–9].
         Recent work, however, brings into question the role of
