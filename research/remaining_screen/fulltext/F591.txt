Mar                       ABSTRACT
24          The mass symmetry between the two species in electron-positron (pair) plasmas has inter-                 esting consequences for collisionless magnetic reconnection because the Hall term, which plays
              a crucial role in supporting fast reconnection in electron-proton plasmas, vanishes. We perform
                 kinetic simulations of pair reconnection in systems of various sizes, show that it remains fast,
             and identify the reason why this occurs. For suﬃciently large systems a Weibel-like temperature
                anisotropy instability develops in the outﬂow from the X-point that causes the current layer to
              broaden and form a Petschek-like open outﬂow. We discuss the parameter regimes in which pair
                reconnection should be fast and the implications for astrophysical pair plasmas.[astro-ph]
